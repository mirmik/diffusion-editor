## Masking and approximate cameras

```bash
PYTHONPATH=. ./venv/bin/python scripts/prepare-pixal3d-qwen-views.py \
  --front /home/mirmik/Vaan/Front.png \
  --qwen-dir .local/pixal3d-multiview/vaan-front-qwen/qwen \
  --output-dir .local/pixal3d-multiview/vaan-front-qwen/views
```

Masks use the editor's U2-Net segmentation worker. Each foreground bounding
box (alpha ≥16) is centered in a square crop/pad whose side is 1.1× foreground
height, then uniformly resized to 1024×1024. Full-size masks, crop coordinates,
and source hashes are saved in `views/masks/` and `views/preparation.json`.
The source files are preserved.

The assumed cameras form a 0/90/180/270-degree orbit at zero elevation, with
20-degree horizontal FOV, distance `1.1 / (2*tan(10°))`, and unit object height.
This matches the nominal foreground occupancy of 1/1.1. They are **estimated
cameras, not recovered/calibrated Qwen cameras**. Bounding-box centering and
equal height do not guarantee true feature correspondence, pose consistency,
or perspective agreement.

replaces all texture and normal-map inputs, retaining the exported vertex

## Results

Both runs succeeded on the RTX 5090. To reproduce the second run, change
`--resolution 1024` to `--resolution 1536` and `run-1024` to `run-1536` in both
commands above. Views, weights, seed, and the default 12-step samplers remain
the same. This is a new HR generation, not subdivision of the first mesh.

| Resolution | Elapsed including load/export | Peak PyTorch allocation | GLB bytes | Triangles |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1024 | 179.00 s | 16.41 GiB | 39,789,348 | 992,835 |
| 1536 | 104.68 s | 25.65 GiB | 38,929,452 | 973,800 |

The second run benefited from warmed file/kernel caches; these one-off timings
do not establish that 1536 is faster. Both exports use the upstream target of
one million triangles and 4096-pixel textures, so final triangle count is not
the resolution of the generative representation.

Open `.local/pixal3d-multiview/vaan-front-qwen/comparison.html` for the four
prepared inputs and side-by-side 1024/1536 PBR, clay, head, and hand views.
The local `build-gallery.py` regenerates this HTML. Original Qwen images and
their contact sheet remain in `qwen/`.

Visual assessment of this object and seed:

- The overall A-pose character, clothing, bags, shoes, and hairstyle are
  recognizable. The additional Qwen views produced a usable full-body result
  with the simple nominal cameras.
- Clay renders confirm actual volumetric hair locks, garment edges, pockets,
  and folds. These details are not only painted into the texture.
- At 1024 the face is smooth and loses fine eye/lip definition. Fingers are
  distinguishable at oblique angles, but their shapes and tips are coarse.
  A frontal hand view alone is misleading because fingers overlap.
- At 1536 some hair/face contours are more pronounced, but the arms show
  stronger faceting and the PBR eyes lose readable iris/pupil detail. This
  run is not uniformly better than 1024. Large planar facets remain on clothes.
- The results are plausible starting meshes, not finished close-up character
  geometry. The experiment does not isolate Qwen inconsistency from camera
  error, the small image footprint of face/hands, and model/postprocess limits.
  No front-only control or calibrated-camera ablation was run on Vaan here.

Remaining detail-quality investigation is tracked in Kanboard #2294.

## Validation and topology

Both GLBs loaded in trimesh and Blender 5.2.1, with finite vertex coordinates,
and each produced 15 review images. Input masks were nonempty, and all four
camera rotation matrices passed orthonormality and positive-determinant checks.
The preparation and rendering scripts passed `py_compile`; `git diff --check`
and all gallery file links passed. An initial renderer compatibility error
with the Eevee enum was fixed and the actual render rerun successfully.

After `merge_vertices(merge_tex=True, merge_norm=True)`, the meshes report:

| Resolution | Boundary edges | Nonmanifold edges | Consistent winding |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1024 | 1 | 6,920 | yes |
| 1536 | 2 | 4,119 | yes |

Neither reports watertight topology. These weld-dependent counts require
localization and do not prove visible holes. Both measurements are added to
existing Kanboard #2290. No repair was applied to the original GLBs.
